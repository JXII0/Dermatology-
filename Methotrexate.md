# Methotrexate

## In one line
Folate antagonist given **ONCE WEEKLY** (psoriasis: 7.5-25 mg/week per the book).

> [!danger] Daily dosing mistake = fatal toxicity.

## ➕ Practical (verify)
- **Folic acid** supplement given on a different day of the week.
- **Teratogenic**: avoid in pregnancy; contraception for both sexes, stop well before conception.
- **Avoid with**: trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (severe marrow suppression), NSAIDs (renal clearance), heavy alcohol, liver disease.
- Antidote for toxicity: **folinic acid**.

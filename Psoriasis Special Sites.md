### 1.Scalp (~50%)

A form of psoriasis affecting the scalp.Seen in about 50% of patients.
![[IMG_4438.jpeg]]

### 2.Nails (all subtypes; fingers 50%, toes 35%)
Common with **PsA** **(especially if DIP joint involvement**).
- **Pitting:** punctate depressions
- **Onycholysis:** nail plate separates from bed
- **Subungual hyperkeratosis:** distal nail-bed keratin. Thickened skin under the nail
- **Oil-drop sign:** yellow-orange discolouration through the plate, **MOST SPECIFIC**
- **Trachyonychia**: rough nails. may occur
> [!danger] DDx of nail pitting: **psoriasis and alopecia areata**.

### 3.Palmoplantar
Plaque **or** pustular type. Often **functionally disabling**. The skin lesions of **reactive arthritis** on the palms and soles look the same.
![[IMG_4424.jpeg]]


### 4.Psoriatic arthritis (PsA, ~30%)
- Arthritis **with psoriasis**; a **seronegative spondyloarthropathy**.
- Affect 30% of psoriasis patients. Any age, most often **30-50**.
- **The most distinctive features:** arthritis of the distal interphalangeal joints and dactylitis
- Stiffness, pain, swelling, tenderness of joints, ligaments, tendons (**dactylitis, enthesitis**) are **Worse after rest, better with movement.
- Skin and joint severity may **not correlate**. **Flares and remissions.**
- X-ray: shows **"cup and saucer"** (metatarsal jammed into base of toe) and **"pencil in cup"** deformity.
- Course: flares and remissions.
> [!success] NOTE:  Dactylitis means a whole finger or toe swollen like a sausage. Enthesitis means inflammation where a tendon meets bone, such as the heel.

### 5.Oral psoriasis
Uncommon; more in severe forms (e.g. pustular). Irregular red patches with raised yellow/white borders (geographic-like) · redness · ulcers · desquamating gingivitis(peeling gums) · and pustules (pustular psoriasis).


### Connections

- **Same sign:** nail pitting with [[Alopecia]]; white nail streaks and pterygium belong to [[Lichen Planus]]; see [[Mucosa Nails and Hair Examination]]
- **Looks like:** palmoplantar psoriasis looks like dyshidrotic eczema and reactive arthritis skin lesions
- **Can lead to:** joint destruction if arthritis is missed

# Phototherapy

## In one line
Controlled UV light: **nbUVB, broadband UVB, PUVA** (psoralen + UVA). Safe, effective, cost-effective; ~**20-25 sessions, 2-3/week** (book).

| Type | Note |
|---|---|
| **nbUVB** | First choice; safe in pregnancy ➕ |
| **BB-UVB** | Older, less used |
| **PUVA** | Topical or oral; long-term skin cancer risk ➕; eye protection after oral psoralen ➕ |

## Used in
[[Psoriasis]] · vitiligo · eczema ➕ (see Backlinks).

## Avoid / caution ➕
Photosensitivity disorders, history of melanoma, photosensitizing drugs.

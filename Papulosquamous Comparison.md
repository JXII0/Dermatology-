### 1. How they look

| Disease | Lesion | Scale | Usual sites | Itch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Psoriasis]] | Well-defined red plaques | Thick, silvery, layered | Scalp, elbows, knees, sacrum; symmetrical | Variable |
| [[Lichen Planus]] | Small flat-topped violet shiny papules, may join into plaques | Little; fine white Wickham striae | Flexor wrists, forearms, shins, mouth, glans | Yes |
| [[Pityriasis Rosea]] | Oval salmon patches: herald patch first, then smaller ones | Fine collarette | Trunk and proximal limbs, Christmas tree pattern; palms and soles spared | Few or none |
| [[Pityriasis Versicolor]] | Round to oval macules, pale or brown | Fine branny | Upper trunk and shoulders | None; cosmetic |
| [[Seborrheic Dermatitis]] | Orange-red patches, sharply edged | Greasy | Scalp, face folds, presternal, body folds | Mild |
| [[Tinea Corporis]] | Ring-shaped patches, raised active edge, clear center | At the edge | Trunk and limbs | Persistent |
| [[Secondary Syphilis]] | Coppery round macules and papules | Sometimes a collarette or psoriasis-like | Generalized, includes palms and soles and mucosa | Not itchy |
| [[Pityriasis Alba]] | Pale, ill-defined, not depigmented | Fine | Cheeks of children | None |

### 2. The clues and the care

| Disease | Key clue | Test | Course | First choice of treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Psoriasis]] | Nail pitting, oil drop, Auspitz sign, Koebner | Biopsy: parakeratosis, Munro microabscesses | Chronic, relapsing | High-potency topical steroid, with or without a vitamin D analog |
| [[Lichen Planus]] | Wickham striae, mouth lace, nail pterygium | Biopsy: hypergranulosis, Civatte bodies | Often settles within a year, leaves dark marks | Corticosteroid, route by type and extent |
| [[Pityriasis Rosea]] | Herald patch, Christmas tree | Clinical; syphilis serology if unsure | Settles in 6 to 8 weeks | Reassurance, symptom relief |
| [[Pityriasis Versicolor]] | Bright yellow Wood’s light; KOH shows spaghetti and meatballs | KOH, Wood’s light | Chronic, recurs | Topical antifungal ➕ |
| [[Seborrheic Dermatitis]] | Greasy scale; commoner in Parkinson disease and HIV ➕ | Clinical | Chronic, relapsing | Ketoconazole shampoo, mild steroid, pimecrolimus |
| [[Tinea Corporis]] | Active scaly edge | KOH: hyphae | Spreads outward | Antifungal |
| [[Secondary Syphilis]] | Palms and soles, condylomata lata, mucous patches, shotty nodes | Syphilis serology | Settles, then latent | Penicillin ➕ (see the syphilis chapter) |
| [[Pityriasis Alba]] | Appears after sun, atopic child | Clinical | Normal color returns in 2 to 3 months | Emollient; steroid ointment for 2 weeks before sun exposure |

### 3. Same test, different answer

| Test | What it shows | Disease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood’s light | Golden yellow | [[Pityriasis Versicolor]] |
| Wood’s light | Coral or deep red | Erythrasma |
| Wood’s light | Brilliant green | Tinea capitis |
| Wood’s light | Bluish white | Favus |
| Wood’s light | Accentuated white | Vitiligo |
| KOH scraping | Hyphae | [[Tinea Corporis]] |
| KOH scraping | Short hyphae and spores (spaghetti and meatballs) | [[Pityriasis Versicolor]] |
| Biopsy | Parakeratosis, Munro microabscesses | [[Psoriasis]] |
| Biopsy | Hypergranulosis, Civatte bodies, band-like infiltrate | [[Lichen Planus]] |
| Serology | VDRL and treponemal tests | [[Secondary Syphilis]] |

# Koebner phenomenon

## In one line
New lesions of the **same disease** appear at sites of skin **trauma** (scratch, surgery, sunburn, tattoo), usually within a couple of weeks ➕.

## Where it appears
✅ [[Psoriasis]] · [[Lichen Planus]] · vitiligo · lichen nitidus ➕
**Pseudo-Koebner** (spread by inoculation, not isomorphic): warts, molluscum ➕

## Don't confuse
- **Auspitz** = bleeding after removing psoriatic scale.
- **Pathergy** = pustule/papule after needle prick (Behçet).

## Practical
Counsel patients to avoid scratching and skin injury.

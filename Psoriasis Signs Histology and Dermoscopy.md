### The three signs, in the order you elicit them
1. **Scrape** → scale comes off like scraping a candle ➕ (**candle-grease**)
2. **Scrape more** → a thin membrane ➕, then **pinpoint bleeding** = **Auspitz sign**. Cause: **enlarged superficial vessels + thinned suprapapillary ridges**.
3. **Trauma** → new lesions in the line of injury = **Koebner phenomenon**.

### Histology (**the biopsy is diagnostic**):

|                                                                                                                        |                                                   |                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Finding                                                                                                                | What it means                                     | What it explains clinically  |
| **Parakeratosis**                                                                                                      | Nuclei retained in the stratum corneum            | Silvery, easily shed scale   |
| **Munro microabscesses**                                                                                               | Collections of neutrophils in the stratum corneum | A classic hallmark           |
| Absent granular layer                                                                                                  | Cells skip maturation                             | Thick scale                  |
| **Acanthosis**, elongated rete ridges ("test-tube")                                                                    | Regular, long epidermal downgrowths               | Plaque thickness             |
| **Thin suprapapillary plates**, Dermis: **dilated, increased, elongated capillaries**; raised, **oedematous papillae** | The dermis is congested                           | Redness and the Auspitz sign |
| Mixed **mononuclear** dermal infiltrate                                                                                | Immune cells                                      | Chronic Inflammation         |

>[!success] NOTE: saw-tooth rete ridges and Civatte bodies belong to **lichen planus**, not psoriasis. Pautrier microabscesses belong to mycosis fungoides. Munro microabscesses are the psoriasis answer.

**Dermoscopy:** whitish areas (scale) with many red dotted blood vessels beneath them, called glomerular vessels. Dry and wet dermoscopy both show it.

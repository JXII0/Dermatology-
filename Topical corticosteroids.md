# Topical corticosteroids

## In one line
High efficacy and safety when used intermittently and supervised (book). **Potency matches the site.**

## ➕ Potency ladder (UK-style, verify local classification)
Very potent: clobetasol → **Potent**: betamethasone valerate, mometasone → Moderate: clobetasone → **Mild**: hydrocortisone 1%.

## Site rule
- **Face, folds, genitals, children** → lowest effective potency.
- **Palms, soles, thick plaques** → potent.
- **Vehicle:** ointment (dry, scaly), cream (moist), lotion/gel/foam (scalp, hair).
- 1 **fingertip unit** (~0.5 g) covers about two adult hand areas ➕.

## Side effects (book)
Skin atrophy, telangiectasia, striae, acne, folliculitis, purpura. ➕ Rebound/pustular flare after stopping potent steroid in psoriasis.

# 🟦 Pityriasis Rosea - Theory

← [[Pityriasis Rosea]] · Practical: [[Pityriasis Rosea management]] · Test: [[Pityriasis Rosea - Exam]]

**Level:** #theory #viva #papulosquamous

**Book pages:** 148 to 149

**Exam echo:** R2 Q25, Q27. R1 Q33.

## §1 Definition, epidemiology and cause

> [!info] Definition
> A **common, acute, self-limited** skin disease.

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Between **10 and 35**; peak in **adolescence** |
| Extremes of age | Rare in infants and old people |
| Race | No predilection |
| Season | **Spring and fall** |
| Cause | A **viral** cause is often proposed. It is associated with **reactivation of HHV-7 or HHV-6** |

## §2 Clinical picture

### The herald patch

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| How often | **80%** of patients |
| When | Appears **hours to days before** the rash |
| Number | **One** lesion (it may be multiple) |
| Site | Usually the **trunk**; less often the neck or proximal limbs |
| Growth | Enlarges over several days |
| Look | **Oval** plaque or patch, **2 to 4 cm**, slightly elevated **advancing margin**, **salmon-red**, **fine collarette scale** at the margin |

### The exanthem (the rash)

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When | **1 to 2 weeks** after the herald patch |
| Look | Many **smaller, thin, scaling** papules and patches with **marginal collarettes**, like the herald patch |
| Color | **Dull pink or tawny** |
| Shape | Round to oval, scattered |
| Pattern | Along the **lines of cleavage**: the **Christmas tree** pattern |
| Sites | **Trunk and proximal arms and legs** |
| Spared | **Face, palms and soles** are usually (but **not always**) spared, **unlike syphilis** |
| Symptoms | Most patients have **few, if any** |

> [!tip] Collarette
> A collarette is a thin ring of scale that is attached at the **outer edge** and free on the **inside**. Both the herald patch and every later lesion have it. ➕ (The explanation of the ring is not in the book.)

## §3 Histology

| Finding | What it is | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patchy or diffuse **parakeratosis** | Nuclei kept in the horny layer | Gives the scale. Shared with psoriasis |
| **Absent granular layer** | The layer is lost | Shared with psoriasis |
| **Psoriasiform hyperplasia** and slight acanthosis | Regular thickening of the epidermis | |
| **Focal spongiosis**, lymphocytic exocytosis and microvesicles | Fluid between the cells | ➕ Explains the vesicles in pityriasis rosea irritata |
| **Red cell extravasation** | Red cells leak out of vessels | |
| Superficial perivascular lymphocytic infiltrate and dermal edema | Inflammation around vessels | |

> [!warning] Pitfall
> The slide shares **parakeratosis** and an **absent granular layer** with psoriasis. So the **clinical picture** decides, and histology rarely does. ➕ See [[Psoriasis - Theory]].

## §4 Atypical pityriasis rosea

- Lesions **only on the face and neck**
- **Herald patch absent**, **multiple**, or the **only** manifestation
- **Pityriasis rosea irritata:** **vesicular** or **simulating erythema multiforme**, caused by **irritation from sweat** or **inadequate treatment**

## §5 Differential diagnosis

| Looks like | How to tell it apart |
| --- | --- |
| **Secondary syphilis** | Coppery, not itchy, palms and soles, mucous patches, nodes. **Obtain serology** |
| **Guttate psoriasis** | **No marginal collarette**; often after a sore throat |
| **Drug eruption** (captopril, barbiturates) | Starts after a drug |
| **Small plaque parapsoriasis** | Chronic, no herald patch ➕ |
| **Erythema multiforme** | Target lesions |
| **Tinea corporis** | KOH shows hyphae. ➕ The herald patch alone can look like tinea |

## §6 Course

- **Spontaneous remission** within **6 to 8 weeks**
- **Recurrences are uncommon** but have been reported
- Most patients have **few, if any,** symptoms
- ➕ No lasting damage; dark or pale marks may follow in darker skin

## §7 Connections

- **Looks like:** [[Secondary Syphilis]], [[Psoriasis]] (guttate), [[Tinea Corporis]], drug eruption, parapsoriasis, erythema multiforme
- **Same sign:** collarette scale
- **Same test:** histology shared with [[Psoriasis]]
- **Triggered by:** HHV-6 and HHV-7 reactivation (suggested)
- **Treated with the same things as:** [[Psoriasis]] and [[Lichen Planus]] (phototherapy). See [[Pityriasis Rosea management]]

**Mnemonic ➕:** *Herald, Collarette, Christmas tree.* And for the course: *6 to 8 weeks, then it is gone.*

**Take-home:** Learn it as a story: one herald patch, then a rash that follows the cleavage lines and spares the palms and soles, then it clears. The look-alike to fear is secondary syphilis.

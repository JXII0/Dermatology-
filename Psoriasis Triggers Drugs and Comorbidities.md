**Triggers:**

- Streptococcal throat or respiratory infection triggers guttate psoriasis
- Stopping corticosteroids triggers pustular psoriasis
- Injury triggers the Koebner phenomenon

**Drugs that worsen psoriasis:**

- Systemic corticosteroids, whether taking or stopping them
- Beta blockers and ACE inhibitors
- Lithium
- Aspirin and antimalarials such as hydroxychloroquine
- Interferons
- NSAIDs
- Sulfa-containing drugs

> [!example] 🧠 LIBAN + S (mnemonic)
> **L**ithium · **I**nterferon · **B**eta-blockers · **A**CE inhibitors / **A**ntimalarials · **N**SAIDs/aspirin · + **S**teroid withdrawal (sulfa also).

**Comorbidities:**

- Cardiovascular risk factors are commoner in moderate to severe psoriasis, so patients should be helped to control the ones they can change
- Higher body mass index goes with more and more severe psoriasis
- Up to 30% develop psoriatic arthritis, which can destroy joints, so ask about joint pain at every visit
- Quality of life is affected. It is a lifelong disease

**Course and prognosis:** chronic and relapsing, with few spontaneous remissions. Guttate psoriasis has a good prognosis. Erythrodermic psoriasis is the worst type.
